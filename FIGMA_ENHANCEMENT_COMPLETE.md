## 🎨 Design System

### Colors
```css
--gradient-cyan: #06b6d4
--gradient-purple: #a855f7
--gradient-pink: #ec4899
--gradient-brand: linear-gradient(135deg, #06b6d4, #a855f7, #ec4899)
```

### Effects
- **Glow Shadows**: `0 0 40px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.3)`
- **Glassmorphism**: `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`
- **Smooth Transitions**: `cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)`

### Animations
- Fade-in-up on scroll
- Parallax scrolling
- Hover scale transforms
- Floating particles
- Pulsing glows
- Counter animations
